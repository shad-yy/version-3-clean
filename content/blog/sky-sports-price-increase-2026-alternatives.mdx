Sky raised their sports subscription prices for the 
third time since 2024. If you received the email, 
you are not alone — and you are asking the right question.

Here is every genuine alternative, 
what each one costs, and whether it actually works 
for Premier League, Champions League, UFC and Formula 1.

## What Sky Sports Costs in 2026

Sky Sports standalone (without a Sky broadband deal): **£43/month**

With a full Sky Entertainment + Sports bundle: £65-85/month depending on deal.

For Sky Sports as a streaming add-on via Sky Glass or NOW TV: £34.99/month for the NOW Sports Month Pass.

Over a year, standalone Sky Sports costs **£516**. 
A Sky bundle costs £780-1,020.

## What You Get For That Price

All Sky Sports channels: Premier League, Main Event, 
Football, F1, Cricket, Golf, Arena, News, Mix.

**Notable omissions at standard price:**  
- Champions League and Europa League: requires TNT Sports (£30.99/month extra)
- NFL Game Pass: separate subscription
- UFC events: occasional PPV charges apply

To watch Premier League AND Champions League on Sky, 
you need Sky Sports (£43) AND TNT Sports (£31) = **£74/month**.

## The Alternatives — Honest Assessment

### Option 1: NOW TV Sports Pass
**Cost:** £34.99/month (Sports Month Pass)  
**What you get:** All Sky Sports channels  
**What you don't get:** TNT Sports, Champions League, no contract flexibility at this price  
**Verdict:** £8 cheaper than standalone Sky Sports. 
Still does not include Champions League. 
Still no contract lock-in, which is a genuine advantage 
over traditional Sky. **Best for: casual viewers 
who watch Sky Sports occasionally.**

### Option 2: TNT Sports via Discovery+
**Cost:** £30.99/month  
**What you get:** TNT Sports 1-4, Champions League, 
Europa League, Premier League (selected fixtures), 
boxsets via discovery+  
**What you don't get:** All Premier League matches 
(Sky holds most), F1, cricket, golf  
**Verdict:** If Champions League is your priority 
and you can live without Sky Sports, this is worth 
considering. **Best for: Champions League-focused viewers.**

### Option 3: Amazon Prime Video
**Cost:** £8.99/month  
**What you get:** A dedicated Premier League matchweek in 
December, included with a standard Prime membership  
**What you don't get:** Any other Premier League fixtures, 
Champions League, F1, cricket or golf  
**Verdict:** Not a Sky replacement on its own, but it covers 
a matchweek neither Sky nor TNT carries. 
**Best for: filling the December gap cheaply.**

## Which Combination Actually Covers What

No single UK service carries every televised Premier League 
match, because the rights are deliberately split.

**Sky Sports** holds the largest package, including most 
Sunday and Monday fixtures and Saturday evening games.

**TNT Sports** holds a smaller Premier League package plus 
the Champions League and Europa League in full.

**Amazon Prime Video** streams one dedicated December matchweek.

**BBC** carries no live Premier League, but *Match of the Day* 
highlights are free and the FA Cup and England internationals 
are shown live under the Ofcom "Listed Events" rules.

Saturday 3pm kick-offs are not televised in the UK at all, 
under the long-standing blackout rule designed to protect 
attendance at lower-league grounds.

## How to Cut the Bill Without Losing Coverage

**Use NOW passes seasonally.** NOW sells Sky Sports content 
on a rolling monthly pass with no contract. Cancelling over 
the summer break and restarting in August saves roughly 
three months of fees each year.

**Drop to TNT Sports alone in a Champions League year.** If 
European football matters more to you than full Premier 
League coverage, this roughly halves the bill.

**Check your Sky contract end date first.** Sky Sports can be 
cancelled at any time after the minimum term, but cancelling 
early usually incurs a fee. Sky Sports and Sky Broadband are 
separate products — check whether a bundle discount ties them 
together before you cancel either.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does Sky Sports keep raising prices?**  
Sky's rights fees for Premier League broadcasts 
increased significantly in the 2025-2028 cycle. 
Sky paid approximately £4.5 billion for UK Premier 
League rights — costs passed directly to subscribers.

**Will the Premier League ever be on a cheaper service?**  
The current Premier League rights deal runs to 2028. 
Sky Sports and TNT Sports hold the UK broadcast rights 
until then, so no cheaper licensed route to the full 
package exists before that cycle ends. The next auction 
is the realistic point at which pricing could change.

**Can I keep my broadband if I cancel Sky Sports?**  
Yes. Sky Sports and Sky Broadband are separate products. 
Cancelling Sky Sports does not affect your broadband 
contract. Check your specific contract terms as some 
bundle deals tie the two together.

**What is the cheapest way to watch televised Premier League matches in 2026?**  
A NOW Sports monthly pass at around £35 is the cheapest 
contract-free route to the Sky Sports package. Adding TNT 
Sports is required for Champions League and TNT's Premier 
League fixtures. Note that no UK service shows all 380 
matches — Saturday 3pm kick-offs are not televised here.
