# How to Watch Premier League 2026-27 Live: Complete TV & Streaming Guide

You can watch televised Premier League 2026-27 matches in the UK on Sky Sports, TNT Sports, and Amazon Prime Video. The rights are split across all three, and roughly 180 of the 380 fixtures — the Saturday 3pm kick-offs — are not broadcast live in the UK at all under the long-standing blackout rule.

The 2026-27 Premier League campaign kicks off on August 21, 2026, and promises to be one of the most competitive seasons yet. With broadcasting rights split across multiple subscription providers, UK football fans face record-high costs to catch every televised fixture. This guide details every official channel, kickoff slot, and the most cost-effective alternatives to watch every match live.

## Official UK TV Rights & Broadcasters for 2026-27

For the 2026-27 season, domestic live broadcasts remain divided between three primary rights holders.

### 1. Sky Sports
Sky Sports remains the dominant broadcaster, showing the majority of live matches.
*   **Fixtures:** 128 live matches per season.
*   **Kickoff Slots:** Friday nights, Saturday 5:30 PM, Sunday 2:00 PM & 4:30 PM, and selected Monday night games.
*   **Cost:** Standalone Sky Sports package starts at £43/month, often requiring bundled TV contracts.

### 2. TNT Sports (formerly BT Sport)
TNT Sports covers the early weekend kickoffs and midweek rounds.
*   **Fixtures:** 52 live matches.
*   **Kickoff Slots:** Saturday 12:30 PM and selected midweek fixtures.
*   **Cost:** £30.99/month via discovery+ or Sky/Virgin add-ons.

### 3. Amazon Prime Video
Amazon retains rights to specific holiday game weeks.
*   **Fixtures:** 20 live matches (two full midweek/bank holiday rounds).
*   **Kickoff Slots:** December bank holidays and early December midweek rounds.
*   **Cost:** £8.99/month (or £95/year for Amazon Prime subscription).

## The Cost Problem: Paying for Three Subscriptions

To legally watch all televised matches in the UK through standard subscription packages, you must subscribe to all three services:

| Broadcaster | Monthly Cost | Live Fixtures |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sky Sports** | £43.00 | 128 matches |
| **TNT Sports** | £30.99 | 52 matches |
| **Amazon Prime** | £8.99 | 20 matches |
| **Total Cost** | **£82.99 / month** | **200 matches (180 blackout)** |

Furthermore, traditional UK broadcasters are subject to the **3 PM Saturday blackout rule**, meaning the remaining 180 matches cannot be shown live on UK television under any circumstances.

---

## How to Cut the Cost Without Losing Coverage

You do not have to hold all three subscriptions at once.

**NOW Sports passes.** NOW sells the Sky Sports package on a rolling monthly basis with no contract. Cancelling across the summer break and restarting in August typically saves around three months of fees per season.

**discovery+ for TNT Sports.** TNT Sports is available through discovery+ on a rolling pass, which also covers the Champions League and Europa League in full.

**Amazon Prime for one matchweek.** The dedicated December round is included with a standard Prime membership, so most households already have it.

**Free-to-air highlights.** *Match of the Day* on BBC One and BBC iPlayer carries highlights of every Saturday fixture at no cost, including the 3pm games no broadcaster may show live.

Prices quoted are the advertised UK rates at the time of writing and change regularly — confirm with each broadcaster before subscribing.
