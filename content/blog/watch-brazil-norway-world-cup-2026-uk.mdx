## How Brazil Got Here

Brazil topped their group with strong performances, confirming their status as one of the pre-tournament favourites:

- Brazil 3–0 Japan (June 14)
- Brazil 2–0 Austria (June 19)
- Brazil 1–1 USA (June 24)

Vinícius Júnior and Rodrygo have been key players in attack, supported by Bruno Guimarães in midfield. The Seleção are aiming to secure their sixth World Cup title and look formidable heading into the knockout rounds.

## How Norway Got Here

Norway qualified for the Round of 16 after finishing second in their group, led by their star striker Erling Haaland:

- Norway 2–1 Morocco (June 16)
- Norway 0–2 France (June 21)
- Norway 3–1 Canada (June 26)

Haaland has already scored four goals in the tournament, making him the focal point of the Norwegian attack. Supported by Martin Ødegaard in midfield, Norway represents a highly structured and dangerous opponent capable of troubling any defence.
