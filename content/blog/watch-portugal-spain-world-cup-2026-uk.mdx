## How Portugal Got Here

Portugal qualified for the Round of 16 after a strong showing in Group F:

- Portugal 2–0 Turkey (June 16)
- Portugal 3–1 Czechia (June 21)
- Portugal 1–2 Georgia (June 26)

Cristiano Ronaldo continues to lead the line for Portugal in what is widely expected to be his final World Cup. Backed by Bruno Fernandes, Bernardo Silva, and Rafael Leão, the Portuguese side possess one of the most potent attacking lineups in the tournament.

## How Spain Got Here

Spain advanced to the knockout phase with dominant possession-based performances in Group B:

- Spain 3–0 Croatia (June 15)
- Spain 1–0 Italy (June 20)
- Spain 2–2 Albania (June 25)

Lamine Yamal and Nico Williams have provided electric pace on the wings, while Rodri controls the tempo from midfield. Spain's high-pressing style and technical quality make them one of the leading contenders to win the trophy.
