## How Mexico Got Here

- Mexico 2–1 Egypt (June 15)
- Mexico 1–1 Switzerland (June 20)

Playing in front of a passionate home crowd at the Estadio Azteca, Mexico will represent a massive test for England. Their speed on the counter-attack and familiarity with the high altitude in Mexico City make them dangerous opponents.
