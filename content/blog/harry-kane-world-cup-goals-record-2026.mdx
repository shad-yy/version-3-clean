Harry Kane became England's all-time top scorer 
at World Cups last night, heading home against Panama 
to score his 11th goal at the tournament — 
surpassing the record of ten held by Gary Lineker 
since the 1986 World Cup in Mexico.

## The Record-Breaking Moment

Kane's header in England's 2-0 win over Panama 
in their final Group L match came after 
Jude Bellingham had opened the scoring.

The goal moved Kane clear as England's greatest 
ever World Cup scorer.

## Kane's World Cup Goal Timeline

Kane first scored at a World Cup in Russia 2018, 
finishing as the tournament's top scorer 
with six goals including a hat-trick against Panama.

In Qatar 2022 he scored three goals in four matches.

In the 2026 tournament in North America he has 
already scored twice in the group stage.
