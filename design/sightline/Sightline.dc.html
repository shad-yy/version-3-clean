1Sightline — art direction, logo, and the three screens

Three directions below. I'd ship 1b. Where I disagree with the brief is marked in the designs with a hairline note: the green isn't the problem so much as having a brand colour at all — on a reference, colour should mean something, not decorate. So in 1b amber only ever means live, blue only ever means film/TV, and "verified" gets no colour at all — just a date in mono. A green tick is exactly the reassurance-theatre this product shouldn't need.

1dLogo — three marks, favicon-proofed
Logo

No play triangle, no television. All three are one path, one colour, and survive 16px. Each is shown at 32px, at 16px against real favicon neighbours, knocked out on amber, and in the lockup.
